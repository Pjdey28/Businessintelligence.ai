INVENTORY OPERATIONS REPORT
Reporting Period: March-April 2025

During April 2025, inventory availability for Product A,
Product B, and Product C remained broadly stable across
North, South, and West regions.

The East region experienced a noticeable deterioration
in Product D availability during the second half of April.

Product D inventory availability declined due to delayed
replenishment from the primary supplier.

The supply interruption resulted in increased stock-out
events for Product D in the East region.

Operations teams initiated replenishment requests, but
normal inventory levels were not fully restored during
the reporting period.

Product D should be monitored closely in the East region
during the following reporting cycle.
