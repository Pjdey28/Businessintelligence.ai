REGIONAL SALES REPORT
Reporting Period: March-April 2025

Overall sales performance remained relatively stable in
North, South, and West.

The East region recorded weaker sales performance during
April 2025.

The decline was concentrated primarily in Product D.

Product D unit sales decreased during the period while
other major products showed comparatively smaller changes.

Sales representatives reported that Product D availability
and delayed fulfillment were recurring concerns among
customers in the East region.

The sales team recommends coordinating with inventory and
operations teams to restore Product D availability.
