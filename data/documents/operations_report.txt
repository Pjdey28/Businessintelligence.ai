OPERATIONS PERFORMANCE REPORT
Reporting Period: March-April 2025

Delivery performance remained stable in the North and
South regions.

The East region experienced higher delivery delays during
April 2025.

The primary operational issue was delayed distributor
fulfillment.

Several distributor shipments containing Product D were
delivered later than the planned delivery date.

The increase in delivery delays coincided with increased
customer complaints related to order fulfillment.

Operations management recommends reviewing distributor
performance and shipment scheduling for the East region.
