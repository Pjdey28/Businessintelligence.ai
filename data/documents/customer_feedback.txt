CUSTOMER FEEDBACK REPORT
Reporting Period: April 2025

Customer complaints increased in the East region during
April 2025.

The most common complaints involved Product D availability,
delayed shipments, and incomplete order fulfillment.

Several customers reported that Product D was unavailable
when attempting to place repeat orders.

Customers affected by delayed distributor shipments also
reported longer-than-expected delivery times.

The feedback indicates that product availability and
delivery reliability were the dominant operational concerns
during the period.
